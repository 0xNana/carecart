---
title: Product roadmap
description: The proposed path from clinic workflow discovery to a repeatable inventory pilot.
---

This roadmap is directional. It has no promised release dates. Priorities will change when clinic teams validate the workflow and the team can support the service safely.

## 1. Validate the workflow

Learn how clinic teams count stock, recognize shortages, get supplier information, approve orders and reconcile delivery. Confirm the most costly problem and who can sponsor a pilot before adding features.

## 2. Prepare a bounded clinic pilot

Agree a small item set, a clinic owner for inventory accuracy, a purchasing approver and the source and freshness of supplier information. Establish access, backups, support and data-handling expectations before using clinic records.

## 3. Measure the pilot

Compare stock accuracy, alert usefulness, order-preparation time, stockout events and delivery discrepancies with a baseline agreed by the clinic. Use the result to decide whether to stop, revise or continue.

## 4. Make onboarding repeatable

If a clinic confirms value, improve setup, catalog loading, training, backup and support so another clinic can use the workflow without custom engineering.

## 5. Expand based on evidence

Supplier integrations, multi-site support and changes to payment automation require demonstrated demand and separate operational, customer and security review. Clinic approval remains required for each real order and payment in the current workflow.
