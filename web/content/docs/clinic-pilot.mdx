title: Clinic pilot
description: What a first CareCart clinic pilot is intended to evaluate.
---

CareCart is preparing to validate its inventory and replenishment workflow with a clinic. This page describes a proposed pilot shape; it is not a claim that a clinic has enrolled or that a pilot date has been set.

## What a pilot would evaluate

- Recording stock receipts, use and adjustments for a clinic-selected set of supplies.
- Whether low-stock signals are accurate and useful to staff.
- How much time it takes to prepare a replenishment order.
- Whether staff can reconcile an order with the physical delivery.

## What would stay under clinic control

Clinic staff would confirm counts, review supplier information, and approve each real purchase and payment. The initial pilot would not make unattended payments or clinical recommendations.

## What a clinic would need to agree

- A staff member responsible for inventory records and an operational sponsor.
- A purchasing approver who can review the proposed workflow.
- A bounded site and item list, with a documented source for supplier prices and availability.
- Baseline and success measures agreed before the pilot starts.
- Data, access, hosting, backup, support and retention terms reviewed before real records are used.

The current website preview is synthetic and does not contact suppliers or place orders. Pilot scope, availability and timing will be published when they are confirmed.
