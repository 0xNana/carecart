CareCart helps clinics keep essential supplies in stock. Staff record what arrives and what is used. CareCart monitors those records, identifies shortages, compares approved offers and creates purchase orders. Eligible purchases can proceed automatically within the clinic's approved items, suppliers and spending limits.

The clinic sets the purchasing authority in advance. Orders outside that authority are held, and staff record physical delivery separately from payment.

## Where we are today

The working demonstration includes inventory management and a completed automatic supplier payment on NEAR testnet. The public preview uses synthetic records; the payment used CCUSD, a CareCart token with no cash value, rather than Circle USDC. It required no human approval for that individual order after the bounded policy was authorized.

Mainnet purchasing, live supplier connections and a paying clinic are the next milestones. None is represented here as already launched or signed. The [readiness guide](/docs/mainnet-readiness) sets out the evidence and remaining decisions.
