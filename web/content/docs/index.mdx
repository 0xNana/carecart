title: CareCart product guide
description: What CareCart does, how clinic replenishment works, and what is planned next.
---

CareCart helps clinic teams keep supply records current and prepare replenishment orders for review. Staff record stock received and used. CareCart can flag low stock and prepare the next order using configured supplier information.

The clinic remains in control: an administrator selects items, approved suppliers and spending limits. Eligible test purchases can proceed automatically; purchases outside the policy stay for review. Real-money purchasing is not enabled.

## Current stage

The public preview uses synthetic clinic and supplier data. It demonstrates an inventory workflow, not a live clinic deployment. A separate bounded run demonstrated a publicly verifiable test-token supplier payment. See the [safety and data guide](/docs/safety-and-data) for the evidence boundaries.
